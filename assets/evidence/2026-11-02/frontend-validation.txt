## Financial cases covered

The USD case reconciles account cash 750 to Andes 350 + Marina 400, with zero belonging to account holder Alex. Tests cover atomic rejection of over-allocation, insufficient account/owner funds, cross-currency payment and overpayment. Partial payment updates both the obligation and its promise; creating/renegotiating promises never changes cash or debt. Paying on behalf of another owner creates a reimbursement receivable.

Invoice line totals and manual taxes create one obligation without a cash entry. Installment principal sums exactly to the original principal, including month-end dates and the last rounding adjustment. Transfers preserve combined cash and ownership. Statement import and reconciliation do not duplicate an existing movement. A request replay returns the original result; conflicting reuse and competing overpayments are rejected. Failed storage writes preserve the previous snapshot, and altered storage is not silently replaced.

## Deliberate limits

This is a browser-only demonstration, not bank reconciliation certification, accounting compliance or real permissions. Budgets are informative spending targets; project tags do not reserve/lock a separate bank balance. Owner/account balance checks control available demo funds. Credit repayments are classified as credit flows and their principal/interest are shown in the schedule; project expense cards summarize operational expense movements. No currency conversion, automatic bank import, uploads of real document content, tax issuance, backend changes, push or deployment were performed.

The API uses queued writes and Web Locks when available; the fallback revision check is a client demo guard rather than a replacement for server transactions. A real deployment still needs backend authorization, persistence, verification and integration.
